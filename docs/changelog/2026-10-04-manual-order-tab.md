# Manual order tab beside the SL desk

## Summary

The trading ticket now has two tabs. SL Order Desk stays the default candle stop form. Manual brings back SL, Limit, and Market placement, including conditional stops, Limit Cancel At, the candle detector, and the market-closed pending-order save.

## Details

- The candle quote fills entry and stop only on the SL Order Desk tab.
- Limit, Market, and conditional edits open the Manual tab. Plain SL edits stay on the desk.
- A market-closed placement can still be saved until Monday 04:30 IST and listed under Pending Orders.
